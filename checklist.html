Melbourne Google listing checklist

A scannable pass over your Google listing and enquire path. Tick what you already have. Fix what’s missing. Public stuff only. No page-one promise attached.

How to use this

Walk it on your phone like a customer would. Search your trade + suburb. Open your listing. Try the call and website buttons. Note one thing you’d fix first.

1. Basics that have to be true

  • Business name matches how customers know you (no keyword stuffing in the title)
  • Primary category is the job you actually want (sparkie, plumber, carpenter), not a vague “contractor”
  • Extra categories only if you really do that work
  • Phone number is correct and taps to dial on a phone
  • Website URL opens, and it’s yours
  • Hours are current (including Saturday if you take weekend jobs)

2. Service area: pins, not a fat circle

Skip thisA 40-50km blob around the depot. Weak signal in every suburb inside it.
Do thisList the Melbourne suburbs you actually drive to. Hide the home address if you’re mobile.
  • Service area lists real suburbs you cover, not a giant radius
  • If you’re a mobile tradie, address is hidden and area shows instead
  • You’re not claiming half of Melbourne “just in case”

3. Services (with real words under them)

  • Every service you quote is listed (not just three generic titles)
  • Each service has a short description in plain English
  • No empty “Switchboard” / “Lighting” titles with nothing underneath

4. Photos and proof you’re real

  • At least a handful of real job photos (phone photos are fine)
  • No stock van / stock handshake images as the main look
  • Logo and cover look finished, not default placeholders
  • Licence or credentials showing if you’ve got them

5. Enquire path (this is where jobs die)

  • Call button dials the right number on a phone
  • Website button opens a live page
  • If you have a quote or contact link, it does not 404
  • On the site, the first screen on a phone makes it obvious how to enquire
  • Forms don’t ask for nine fields before someone can send

6. Who sits above you (quick look)

  • You searched your main trade + one suburb you care about
  • You noted who appears above you in the map pack
  • You checked their category, photo count, and whether their call link works
  • You picked one gap on your listing you can close this week

7. Reviews (honest only)

  • You know how to ask a happy customer for a Google review after the job
  • You’re not buying reviews or swapping fake ones
  • Old leftover Q&A on the listing has been cleaned if it’s still sitting there
If you don’t have a listing yet

Claim it in Google Business Profile. Google may verify with a postcard to the business address, or sometimes phone or video. Finish verification before you polish every field. Same checklist applies once you’re in.
